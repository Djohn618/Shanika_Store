Two generations, one family
Our story

It started with wood, coconut leaves and a car battery.

In 2014, Bandu Kumara built a small vegetable stall on a five-perch plot near the roundabout and named it after his wife, Shanika. No electricity, no water, just three LED bulbs and a lot of hard work.

Today the shop is run by Nipuna Sadaruwan Alenthuge and supplies families, companies and government institutions across Trincomalee.
