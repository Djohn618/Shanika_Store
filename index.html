how it startedWood, coconut leaves and a car battery.

In 2014, Bandu Kumara John Kothalawalage bought a small five-perch piece of land near the roundabout. With his wife and a few family members he built a vegetable stall out of wood and coconut leaves, and named it after his wife, Shanika.

There was no electricity and no water. A car battery kept three LED bulbs going in the evenings. Every two months he travelled to Dambulla for vegetables, and slowly the stall started selling rice, beans and sugar too.

If you have a goal or a dream, never let others discourage you. Work hard, stay consistent and keep a kind heart. Success comes to those who never give up.

Bandu Kumara John Kothalawalage

Today the shop is run by his brother-in-law, Nipuna Sadaruwan Alenthuge, and it's busier than ever.
