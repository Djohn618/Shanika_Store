Privacy policy

What happens with your data when you visit shanikastore.com, in plain language.

This page is only available in English.

Last updated: 27 October 2026

1. Who is responsible

This website is run for Shanika Super & Grocery, No. 119, Abayapura Junction, Kandy Road, Trincomalee, Sri Lanka. For any question about your data, write to nipunasan91@gmail.com.

2. The short version

  • We don't use advertising, tracking or analytics cookies.
  • We only receive personal data if you send it to us through a form or by e-mail.
  • We never sell your data or use it for marketing.
  • Google Maps loads only after you click "Load map".

3. Visiting the website (hosting)

The website is hosted by Vercel Inc. (USA). When you open a page, your browser automatically sends technical data such as your IP address, the date and time, the page requested, and your browser and device type. Vercel processes this data to deliver the website and keep it secure, and keeps server logs only for a short time. We do not combine this data with other sources.

4. Contact and feedback forms

If you use our contact or feedback form, we receive the details you enter (for example your name, e-mail address, phone number and message, or your rating and feedback). We use them only to answer you or to improve the shop.

The forms are delivered to our e-mail inbox by the service FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), and the e-mails are stored with Google (Gmail). We keep messages only as long as needed to deal with your request, and delete them after at most two years unless the law requires us to keep them longer.

5. Fonts

To display text correctly, the website loads fonts from Google Fonts (Google Ireland Ltd. / Google LLC). For this, your browser connects to Google's servers, which can see your IP address. Google Fonts does not set cookies.

6. Google Maps

The map on our "Visit & contact" page is provided by Google. It is not loaded automatically. Only when you click "Load map" does your browser connect to Google, which may then set cookies and process your IP address and usage data under Google's privacy policy. If you tick "Always load maps", we remember that choice in your browser.

Links to Google Maps for directions or reviews open Google's own website, where Google's privacy policy applies.

7. Settings stored in your browser

We use your browser's local storage (not cookies) to remember a few preferences on your own device: your chosen language, your map choice and the time of your last form submission. This data never leaves your device and you can delete it at any time in your browser settings.

8. Your rights

Under Sri Lanka's Personal Data Protection Act, No. 9 of 2022 (and, for visitors from the EU or Switzerland, the GDPR and the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection), you can ask us:

  • what personal data we hold about you,
  • to correct or delete it,
  • to stop or limit using it, and
  • to withdraw any consent you gave, for the future.

Just e-mail nipunasan91@gmail.com. You also have the right to complain to a data protection authority, such as the Data Protection Authority of Sri Lanka.

9. Changes

We may update this policy when the website changes. The current version is always available on this page.
